רוח נכאה

Neither the word optimism nor pessimism appears in the Bible. Both are modern terms derived from Latin (optimus meaning "best" and pessimus meaning "worst") that entered everyday vocabulary centuries after the biblical texts were written and translated. However, Scripture extensively addresses both outlooks through concepts like hope, faith, trust, and joy versus despair (ייאוש), anxiety (חרדה), cynicism (ציניות), and a "downcast" (מדוכדך, רוח נכאה) spirit. Rather than framing them as fixed personality traits, the Bible treats them as spiritual postures (עמדות), acknowledging that reality is often harsh while calling believers to ground their expectations in God's character rather than their immediate circumstances.
